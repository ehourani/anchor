Anchor

Privacy Policy

Effective June 30, 2026

Welcome to Anchor. Anchor is a personal self-help tool designed to help you organize healthy coping skills, create a crisis toolkit, and reflect on what supports your wellbeing. We understand that the information you choose to store in Anchor may be personal or sensitive, and we are committed to handling it responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, how long we retain it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you create and use an Anchor account, you may provide:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if you choose to add one.
  • If you sign in with Google, your basic Google account information (such as your name and email address) needed to create and authenticate your account.

You may also choose to store information within the app, including:

  • Coping skills you create, including titles, descriptions, and tags.
  • Your crisis toolkit.
  • Reflections about your use of coping skills, including timestamps, optional helpfulness ratings, and any notes you choose to write.

You are never required to include medical or personally identifying information in your reflections, although you may choose to do so.

Technical Information

To operate the service, we also process limited technical information, such as:

  • Authentication and session information needed to keep you signed in.
  • Basic server logs generated while providing the service.
  • Information necessary to diagnose technical issues or maintain security.

We do not use advertising technologies, analytics platforms, behavioral tracking, or third-party advertising cookies.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, health metrics, or other device information except as necessary to operate the app or as you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve Anchor. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Authenticate your sign-in.
  • Store and display your coping skills, crisis toolkit, and reflections.
  • Enable features such as data export and account deletion.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not:

  • Sell your personal information.
  • Share your information for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Use your information to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Allow other users to view your information.

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers to operate Anchor. These providers process information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

Our current providers include:

  • Supabase, which provides authentication and database services. Data is hosted on infrastructure located in the United States.
  • Vercel, which hosts the application.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in using your Google account.

These providers may themselves rely on infrastructure subcontractors, such as cloud hosting providers. Where applicable, we require our providers to process information only for the purpose of providing services to Anchor.

How Information Is Stored

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted through Supabase in the United States.

We use technical safeguards designed to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit.
  • Authentication controls.
  • Database row-level security to isolate each user's data from other users.
  • Access controls designed to limit administrative access.

Although we use reasonable safeguards, no method of transmitting information over the Internet or storing electronic information can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

When Information May Be Shared

We do not sell or publicly disclose your personal information.

Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that process information on our behalf to operate Anchor.
  • If required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or lawful governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of Anchor, our users, or others.
  • If you choose to export or otherwise share your own information.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active.

If you delete your account, your information is removed from the production database. Deletion is intended to be permanent and cannot be undone.

At this time, we do not maintain a separate backup retention period beyond standard operational recovery mechanisms used to maintain service reliability. Those mechanisms are not intended to restore individual deleted accounts.

Limited technical records may be retained where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or protect the security of the service.

Your Choices

You can manage your information at any time through the Account & Data section of the app.

You may:

  • Export your data in JSON or CSV format.
  • Change your password.
  • Permanently delete your account and associated information.

Deleting your account permanently removes your data as described in this Privacy Policy.

Business Transfers

If Anchor is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of some or all of its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and this Privacy Policy or a successor policy.

Children's Privacy

Anchor is intended for users who are at least 13 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

International Users

Anchor is operated from the United States, and your information will be processed and stored in the United States. If you access Anchor from another country, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Safety Notice

Anchor is a self-help and organizational tool. It is not a medical device, is not intended to provide medical, psychological, or clinical advice, and is not an emergency service.

Anchor does not actively monitor user content for signs of crisis, provide emergency intervention, or contact emergency services on your behalf.

If you believe you are experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, call your local emergency services immediately. In the United States and Canada, you can also contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make material changes, we will update the Effective Date above and provide notice within the app before or when the changes become effective where appropriate.

Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict-of-law principles.
